## The rule

Adverbs of frequency tell us **how often** something happens. We usually use them with the **present simple**.

<ul class="skala" aria-label="Frequency scale">
  <li style="--p:100%"><b>always</b><span>100 %</span></li>
  <li style="--p:90%"><b>usually</b><span>90 %</span></li>
  <li style="--p:70%"><b>often</b><span>70 %</span></li>
  <li style="--p:50%"><b>sometimes</b><span>50 %</span></li>
  <li style="--p:20%"><b>not often / rarely</b><span>20 %</span></li>
  <li style="--p:0%"><b>never</b><span>0 %</span></li>
</ul>

## Where do they go?

<div class="pravidla">
<div class="pravidlo"><p class="pravidlo__nazov">1 · Before the main verb</p>
<p>I <mark>usually</mark> get up at seven.<br>My dad <mark>often</mark> cooks dinner.</p></div>
<div class="pravidlo"><p class="pravidlo__nazov">2 · After <em>be</em></p>
<p>She <em>is</em> <mark>always</mark> happy.<br>We <em>are</em> <mark>never</mark> bored at the weekend.</p></div>
<div class="pravidlo"><p class="pravidlo__nazov">3 · After an auxiliary verb</p>
<p>I <em>can</em> <mark>never</mark> remember his name.<br>They <em>don't</em> <mark>often</mark> play tennis.</p></div>
</div>

## Questions

Put the adverb after the subject: *Do you **often** read comics?* To ask about frequency, use **How often…?**

- *How often do you visit your grandma?* – *About twice a month.*

## Watch out

- **Never** is already negative. Don't use it with *don't* or *doesn't*.
- **Sometimes** can also start the sentence: *Sometimes I walk to school.*
