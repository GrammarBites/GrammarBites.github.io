Sloveso **be** (byť) má v prítomnom čase tvary *am, is, are*. V minulom čase má len dva tvary: **was** a **were**.

## Tvary

<div class="pravidla">
<div class="pravidlo"><p class="pravidlo__nazov">I / he / she / it</p>
<p><mark>was</mark> · <mark>wasn't</mark> (was not)</p></div>
<div class="pravidlo"><p class="pravidlo__nazov">we / you / they</p>
<p><mark>were</mark> · <mark>weren't</mark> (were not)</p></div>
</div>

**Pomôcka:** *am / is* → **was**, *are* → **were**.

## Oznamovacia veta

- *I was at home yesterday.* – Včera som bol doma.
- *My friends were at the zoo last Sunday.* – Kamaráti boli minulú nedeľu v zoo.
- *It was sunny.* – Bolo slnečno.

## Zápor

- *It wasn't cold.* – Nebola zima.
- *We weren't at school on Monday.* – V pondelok sme neboli v škole.

Pri slovese *be* sa v zápore nepoužíva *didn't*: ~~I didn't be~~ → *I wasn't*.

## Otázka a krátka odpoveď

**Was / were** dáme na začiatok vety.

<table class="chyby tvary">
<thead><tr><th scope="col">otázka</th><th scope="col">áno</th><th scope="col">nie</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td><b>Was</b> it fun?</td><td>Yes, it <b>was</b>.</td><td>No, it <b>wasn't</b>.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>Was</b> he here?</td><td>Yes, he <b>was</b>.</td><td>No, he <b>wasn't</b>.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>Were</b> you at the party?</td><td>Yes, I <b>was</b>. / Yes, we <b>were</b>.</td><td>No, I <b>wasn't</b>. / No, we <b>weren't</b>.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>Were</b> they at home?</td><td>Yes, they <b>were</b>.</td><td>No, they <b>weren't</b>.</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

- *Where were you yesterday?* – Kde si bol včera?
- *Was it fun?* – Bolo to zábavné?

Pozor: na otázku *Were **you**…?* odpovedáme podľa toho, kto odpovedá: *Yes, **I was**.* (ja) alebo *Yes, **we were**.* (my).

## There was / There were

- *There was a big dog.* – Bol tam veľký pes.
- *There were three cats.* – Boli tam tri mačky.
- *Was there a pool? – Yes, there was.* – Bol tam bazén? – Áno, bol.

## Kedy?

Minulý čas prezrádzajú časové výrazy: *yesterday, last week, last Saturday, two days ago, in 2020*. Viac ich nájdeš v lekcii [Time expressions](/lekcie/time-expressions/).
