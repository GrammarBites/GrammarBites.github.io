## Teraz × vtedy

Keď hovoríme o **minulosti** (čo bolo), namiesto *am, is, are* povieme <span class="f-okrova">was</span> alebo <span class="f-ruzova">were</span>.

<table class="chyby tvary">
<thead><tr><th scope="col">teraz</th><th scope="col">vtedy (minulosť)</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>I am, he is, she is</td><td>I <span class="f-okrova">was</span>, he <span class="f-okrova">was</span>, she <span class="f-okrova">was</span></td></tr>
<tr><td>you are, we are, they are</td><td>you <span class="f-ruzova">were</span>, we <span class="f-ruzova">were</span>, they <span class="f-ruzova">were</span></td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<ul class="farebne-vety">
<li><span class="f-modra">I</span> <span class="f-okrova">was</span> <span class="f-fialova">happy.</span><small>Bol som šťastný.</small></li>
<li><span class="f-modra">You</span> <span class="f-ruzova">were</span> <span class="f-fialova">kind.</span><small>Bol si milý.</small></li>

</ul>

<ul class="farebne-vety">
