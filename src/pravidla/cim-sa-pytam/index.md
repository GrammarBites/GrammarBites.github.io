Na otázku *áno / nie* odpovedáme krátko. Zopakujeme **to sloveso, ktorým otázka začína** – nie hlavné sloveso.

<table class="chyby tvary">
<thead><tr><th scope="col">otázka</th><th scope="col">áno</th><th scope="col">nie</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td><b>Can</b> you swim?</td><td>Yes, I <b>can</b>.</td><td>No, I <b>can't</b>.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>Do</b> you like Maths?</td><td>Yes, I <b>do</b>.</td><td>No, I <b>don't</b>.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>Is</b> she your sister?</td><td>Yes, she <b>is</b>.</td><td>No, she <b>isn't</b>.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>Did</b> you see the match?</td><td>Yes, I <b>did</b>.</td><td>No, I <b>didn't</b>.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>Have</b> you ever been to London?</td><td>Yes, I <b>have</b>.</td><td>No, I <b>haven't</b>.</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

## Pozor na osobu

Sloveso zopakujeme, ale **osobu** prispôsobíme tomu, kto odpovedá:

- *Are **you** tired? – Yes, **I am**.*
- *Do **you** (vy) live here? – Yes, **we do**.*
