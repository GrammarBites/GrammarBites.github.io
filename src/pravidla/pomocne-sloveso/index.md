V slovenčine stačí zmeniť intonáciu (*Bývaš v Martine?*) alebo pridať *ne-* (*nebývam*). V angličtine potrebujeme **pomocné sloveso**.

<div class="pravidla">
<div class="pravidlo"><p class="pravidlo__nazov">otázka: pomocné sloveso pred podmet</p>
<p><mark>Do</mark> you live in Martin?<br><mark>Can</mark> you swim?<br><mark>Is</mark> she at home?</p></div>
<div class="pravidlo"><p class="pravidlo__nazov">zápor: pomocné sloveso + not</p>
<p>I <mark>don't</mark> live in Martin.<br>I <mark>can't</mark> swim.<br>She <mark>isn't</mark> at home.</p></div>
</div>

## Ktoré pomocné sloveso?

<table class="chyby tvary">
<thead><tr><th scope="col">vo vete je…</th><th scope="col">otázka</th><th scope="col">zápor</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>am / is / are, was / were</td><td><b>Are</b> you…? <b>Was</b> it…?</td><td>I'm <b>not</b>…, it <b>wasn't</b>…</td></tr>
<tr><td>can, must, will…</td><td><b>Can</b> you…? <b>Must</b> I…?</td><td>I <b>can't</b>…, you <b>mustn't</b>…</td></tr>
<tr><td>have (present perfect)</td><td><b>Have</b> you seen…?</td><td>I <b>haven't</b> seen…</td></tr>
<tr><td>iné sloveso (present / past simple)</td><td><b>Do / Does / Did</b> you…?</td><td>I <b>don't / doesn't / didn't</b>…</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

Ak vo vete nie je žiadne pomocné sloveso, „požičiame“ si **do / does / did**.
