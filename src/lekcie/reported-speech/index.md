**Priama reč** (*direct speech*) sú presné slová, ktoré niekto povedal – v úvodzovkách. **Nepriama reč** (*reported speech*) je, keď ich **prerozprávame** niekomu ďalšiemu.

- *„I like pizza,“ says Tom.* – priama reč
- *Tom says (that) he likes pizza.* – nepriama reč

Slovo **that** (že) môžeš vynechať.

## says – čas sa nemení

Keď je uvádzacie sloveso v prítomnom čase (*says*), čas ostane rovnaký. Zmení sa len **osoba**:

- *„I'm tired.“* → *Anna **says** she**'s** tired.*

## Zámená sa menia

Mysli na to, **kto hovorí** a **o kom**:

- *„**I** love **my** dog.“* → *Anna said **she** loved **her** dog.*
- *„**We** are late.“* → *They said **they** were late.*
- *„I'll help **you**.“* → *He said he would help **me**.* (ak to povedal mne)

## said – čas o krok späť

Keď je uvádzacie sloveso v minulom čase (*said*), sloveso v nepriamej reči sa zvyčajne **posunie o krok do minulosti**:

<table class="chyby tvary">
<thead><tr><th scope="col">priama reč</th><th scope="col">nepriama reč</th><th scope="col">príklad</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>present simple</td><td><b>past simple</b></td><td>„I live in Martin.“ → He said he <b>lived</b> in Martin.</td></tr>
<tr><td>present continuous</td><td><b>past continuous</b></td><td>„I'm watching TV.“ → She said she <b>was watching</b> TV.</td></tr>
<tr><td>will</td><td><b>would</b></td><td>„I'll call you.“ → He said he <b>would</b> call me.</td></tr>
<tr><td>can</td><td><b>could</b></td><td>„I can swim.“ → She said she <b>could</b> swim.</td></tr>
<tr><td>am / is / are going to</td><td><b>was / were going to</b></td><td>„We're going to win.“ → They said they <b>were going to</b> win.</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

V slovenčine sa čas nemení (*Povedal, že býva v Martine.*), v angličtine áno (*He said he **lived** in Martin.*).

Zopakuj si minulé tvary v lekciách [Past simple](/lekcie/past-simple/) a [Past continuous](/lekcie/past-continuous/). Ako zmeniť *will* na *would* a *can* na *could*, poznáš aj z lekcií [Second conditional](/lekcie/second-conditional/) a [Past modals](/lekcie/past-modals/).

## said × told

- *He **said** (that) he was tired.* – za *said* nie je osoba
- *He **told me** (that) he was tired.* – za *told* je vždy osoba (komu)
