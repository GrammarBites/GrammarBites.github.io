V slovenčine máme jeden prítomný čas: *hrám*. V angličtine máme dva a musíme si vybrať: robím to **pravidelne**, alebo **práve teraz**? Táto lekcia porovnáva [present simple](/lekcie/present-simple/) a [present continuous](/lekcie/present-continuous/).

## Kedy ktorý?

<div class="pravidla">
<div class="pravidlo"><p class="pravidlo__nazov">present simple = pravidelne, zvyky, fakty</p>
<p>I <mark>play</mark> football every Saturday.<br>He <mark>plays</mark> football every Saturday.</p></div>
<div class="pravidlo"><p class="pravidlo__nazov">present continuous = práve teraz, dočasne</p>
<p>I'<mark>m doing</mark> my homework now.<br>He <mark>is doing</mark> his homework now.</p></div>
</div>

- *I play football every Saturday.* – Každú sobotu hrám futbal.
- *I'm doing my homework now.* – Práve si robím úlohu.
- *Penguins live in cold places.* – Tučniaky žijú v chladných krajinách. (fakt → present simple)

## Signálne slová

<table class="chyby tvary">
<thead><tr><th scope="col">present simple</th><th scope="col">present continuous</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>every day / week / Saturday</td><td>now</td></tr>
<tr><td>on Mondays, at the weekend</td><td>at the moment</td></tr>
<tr><td>always, usually, often, sometimes, never</td><td>today</td></tr>
<tr><td>in the morning, after school</td><td>Look! Listen! Shh!</td></tr>
<tr><td>regularly</td><td>in this picture</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

Frekvenčné príslovky (*always, usually…*) a kam ich vo vete dať, nájdeš v lekcii [Adverbs of frequency](/lekcie/adverbs-of-frequency/).

## Porovnaj

- *Tom **usually walks** to school. But **today** he**'s going** by bus.* – Tom zvyčajne chodí do školy pešo. Ale dnes ide autobusom.
- *Mum **works** in a bank. **Now** she**'s having** lunch.* – Mama pracuje v banke. Teraz obeduje.

## Zápor a otázka

<table class="chyby tvary">
<thead><tr><th scope="col"></th><th scope="col">present simple</th><th scope="col">present continuous</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>zápor</td><td>He <b>doesn't play</b>.</td><td>He <b>isn't playing</b>.</td></tr>

## Pozor na to

- **Slovesá bez -ing:** *like, love, hate, know, want, need* bývajú len v present simple, aj keď hovoríme o „teraz“: *I **like** this song.* Nie ~~I'm liking this song.~~ *I **know** the answer.* Nie ~~I'm knowing~~.
- **-s pri he / she / it** patrí len do present simple (*he plays*). V present continuous je *-s* v slovese *is* (*he is playing*).
- **Nemiešaj pomocné slovesá:** ~~Do you playing?~~ → *Do you play?* alebo *Are you playing?*
